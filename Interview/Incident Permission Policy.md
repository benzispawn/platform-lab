*"The **trust policy** is attached to the role and says **who** may assume it: the principal and the `sts:AssumeRole` action. The **permission policy** says **what** the role's sessions can do, meaning which actions on which resources. My incident was in the permission policy. I know that because `assume-role` succeeded, so trust was fine, and `ListBucket` worked while `GetObject` return 403. The action was right but the resource ARN was the wrong type.*
